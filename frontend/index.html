Campus Regulations & Attendance Guidelines

1. Minimum Attendance Requirement

Under University Regulation Section 4.2, every student must maintain a minimum of 75.0% attendance in each registered course to be eligible to appear for the End-Semester Examinations.

2. Condonation of Shortage

Shortage between 65.0% and 74.9% may be condoned by the Academic Council solely on medical grounds with valid hospital admission proofs submitted within 3 working days.

3. On-Duty (OD) Leave

Attendance exemption for inter-collegiate competitions, hackathons, or official conferences requires prior approval from the Head of Department and Dean.
